## What it says
NeOnc paid cash to buy back all 6,000 shares of its Series A Convertible Preferred Stock on September 15, 2026, using part of the money from its September offering. It then filed paperwork in Delaware to cancel that class of stock. The press release says this removed the chance that the preferred shares would later convert into common stock at a discount.

## Key numbers and dates (quoted)
- 8-K Item 3.03: "redeemed all outstanding shares ... at a redemption price of $965 per share, which amounted to a cumulative redemption amount of approximately $5,790,000"
- Press release: "redeemed for cash all 6,000 outstanding shares ... at their aggregate stated value of $6.0 million"
- Original issue: "NeOnc issued the Series A Preferred Stock in June 2026 in a private placement for gross proceeds of $5.0 million"
- Certificate of Designation filed "June 10, 2026"; "six thousand (6,000) shares" issued (Ex. 3.1)
- Redemption date: "September 15, 2026"; board resolution "September 17, 2026" (Ex. 3.1); Certificate of Elimination filed "September 18, 2026" (8-K Item 5.03)
- Terms if not redeemed: "the stated value would have increased by $166.67 per share, and the shares would have become convertible ... at a conversion price equal to 80% of the lowest closing price during the five trading days prior to conversion, subject to a $1.00 floor price"
- Redemption right: "within four months of issuance"

## Source type
Company-reported. Items 3.03 and 5.03 are "filed" statements; the press release (Ex. 99.1) is "furnished". Ex. 3.1 is a corporate legal document signed by the CEO. No independent source.

## Promotional language and missing numbers
- **Promotional:** the headline "Eliminates Related Potential Dilution"; "disciplined management of shareholder capital"; "translating clinical progress into lasting value".
- **Missing:** the net proceeds of the $15 million offering, how much cash remains after the redemption, and the stated value per share.
