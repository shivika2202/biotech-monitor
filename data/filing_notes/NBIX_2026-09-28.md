# NBIX 8-K filed 2026-09-28

- **Company:** Neurocrine Biosciences, Inc. (CIK 914475)
- **Filing date:** 2026-09-28 (period of report 2026-09-25)
- **Items:** 5.02 (departure or appointment of officers; compensatory arrangements)
- **Source:** 8-K https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/914475/000091447526000045/nbix-20260925.htm
- **Main exhibit:** none attached. The 8-K says the Company "anticipates filing" the Employment Agreement "with its Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q for the three months ending September 30, 2026".
- **Notes written:** 2026-10-17

## What it says
Neurocrine changed its Chief Medical Officer. Sanjay Keswani, M.D.'s service as CMO ended on September 25, 2026, by mutual agreement with the Board, and he is entitled to severance under the company's Executive Severance Plan. Eiry W. Roberts, M.D. was appointed CMO effective September 28, 2026, under an amended and restated employment agreement. The filing gives the new CMO's salary, target bonus and the target values of equity grants.

## Key numbers and dates (quoted)
- Appointment: "On September 28, 2026, the Board of Directors (the "Board") of Neurocrine Biosciences, Inc. (the "Company") appointed Eiry W. Roberts, M.D., to succeed Sanjay Keswani, M.D., as the Company's Chief Medical Officer, effective September 28, 2026"
- Departure: "On September 25, 2026, Dr. Keswani and the Board mutually agreed that Dr. Keswani's service as the Company's Chief Medical Officer would terminate as of September 25, 2026."
- Severance: "Dr. Keswani will be entitled to the severance benefits set forth in Section 4.1 of the Company's Executive Severance Plan, effective February 7, 2025"
- Salary and bonus: "an annual base salary of $825,000 and will be eligible to receive an annual cash incentive bonus with a target bonus amount equal to 60% of her base pay earned for calendar year 2026"
- Equity: "stock options with a target grant value of approximately $625,000"; "restricted stock units with a target grant value of approximately $250,000"; "performance-vesting restricted stock units with a target grant value of approximately $375,000"
- Grant timing: "The Equity Grants will automatically be granted to Dr. Roberts on the date that is two business days following the filing of the Company's first quarterly report on Form 10-Q following the Transition Date"
- Prior agreement: "previously entered into with Dr. Roberts on May 30, 2025, as it was previously amended on November 21, 2025"

## Source type
Company-reported. Item 5.02 is a "filed" statement; there is no press release in this filing.

## Promotional language and missing numbers
- **Promotional language:** none.
- **Missing:** the reason for the change (the filing says only "mutually agreed"); the dollar amount of Dr. Keswani's severance (the filing points to the Severance Plan but gives no figure); the number of shares in the equity grants (set later "by reference to the price of the Company's common stock on the grant date"); and the performance conditions for the performance-vesting units.
- **Not stated:** Dr. Roberts' role at the company before this appointment. The filing shows only that an employment agreement with her already existed.
