## What it says
Director Steven L. Giannotta, M.D., resigned from the board on October 9, 2026. Ali Mohammad Ahmad Alabedin, Group Head of Islamic Banking at First Abu Dhabi Bank, was appointed in his place the same day. The press releases tie the appointment to NeOnc's plans in Abu Dhabi. They say the Department of Health in Abu Dhabi has authorized NEO100 and NEO212 for clinical development there, and that the CEO will speak at the Abu Dhabi Future Health Summit, October 20 to 22, 2026.

## Key numbers and dates (quoted)
- Resignation: "On October 9, 2026, Steven L. Giannotta, M.D., a Class I director, notified ... of his resignation ... effective October 9, 2026"
- Reason: "Dr. Giannotta's resignation was not the result of a disagreement with the Company"
- Appointment: "Effective October 9, 2026 ... Mr. Alabedin will serve as a Class I director until the Company's annual meeting of stockholders in 2029"
- Committees: "The Board has not appointed Mr. Alabedin to any committees"
- Background (8-K): "From August 2022, Mr. Alabedin has been Group Head of Islamic Banking with First Abu Dhabi Bank"
- Bank (Ex. 99.1): "the UAE's largest bank ... with AED 1.41 trillion in total assets as of June 30, 2026"
- Abu Dhabi NEO212 (Ex. 99.1): "The Department of Health – Abu Dhabi has granted IND authorization for NEO212 ... subject to required ethics approvals and other pre-enrollment conditions"
- Abu Dhabi NEO100 (Ex. 99.2): "In June 2026, NeOnc announced that the Department of Health – Abu Dhabi had granted Investigational New Drug (IND) authorization for NEO100, covering the NEO100-01, NEO100-02 and NEO100-03 protocols ... The authorized indication is progressive or recurrent Grade III or IV gliomas"
- Summit: "October 20 to 22 at the ADNEC Centre in Abu Dhabi"; panel "On Thursday, October 22"
