- **Company:** Ultragenyx Pharmaceutical Inc. (CIK 1515673)
- **Filing date:** 2026-10-07 (period of report 2026-10-06)

- **Source:** 8-K https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1515673/000119312526416251/rare-20261006.htm

- **Notes written:** 2026-10-15

## What it says
Ultragenyx agreed to sell a Rare Pediatric Disease Priority Review Voucher (PRV) for $210 million in cash, paid at closing. A PRV is an FDA voucher that speeds up review of a later drug application, and it can be sold. Ultragenyx received this one when the FDA approved GENGLYCOS (DTX401) for glycogen storage disease type Ia. 20% of the gross proceeds go to the NIH under a 2018 patent license, and the sale still has to clear an antitrust waiting period.

## Key numbers and dates (quoted)
- Agreement date: "On October 6, 2026"
- Voucher origin: "awarded the PRV by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration ("FDA") on August 19, 2026 upon approval of GENGLYCOS™ (pariglasgene brecaparvovec-opnr), also known as DTX401, in adult and pediatric patients eight years and older with glycogen storage disease type Ia (GSDIa)"
- Price: "the buyer agreed to pay the Company $210 million, payable in cash, upon the closing of the transaction"
- NIH share: "20% of the gross proceeds from the sale of the PRV will be payable by the Company to the NIH"
- NIH license date: "dated December 10, 2018"
- Condition: "the expiration or termination of the applicable waiting period under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976"
