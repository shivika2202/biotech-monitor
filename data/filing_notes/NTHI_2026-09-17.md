## What it says
The CEO, Amir F. Heshmatpour, bought 35,000 more NeOnc shares on the open market on September 15, 2026. The press release says he and the Chief Medical Officer have together bought 160,016 shares since the company reported Phase 2a results for NEO100 on August 12, 2026. The release also repeats those Phase 2a results. Item 7.01 material is "furnished", not "filed", so it carries less legal liability than a filed statement.

## Source type
Company-reported (press release furnished as an 8-K exhibit). No independent source is cited.

## Promotional language and missing numbers
- **Promotional:** "Positive NEO100 Phase 2a Results" in the headline; "The strength of our Phase 2a results reinforces my conviction"; "pioneering technology"; "multi-Phase 2 clinical-stage".
- **Caveats the release itself states:** the trial had no randomized control arm, comparisons to historical data are "inherently limited", and it "was not powered as a confirmatory trial".
- **Missing numbers:** the release says "the primary efficacy analysis presented reflects RANO 2.0 criteria and Kaplan-Meier estimation, and results differ under the response criteria and estimation method specified in the study protocol." The results under the protocol-specified method are not given.
- **Also missing:** the source of the "20% benchmark", and safety numbers.
