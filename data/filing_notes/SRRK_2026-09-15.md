## What it says
This 8-K corrects a statement management made on a September 14, 2026 investor call about the FDA approval of ISEMBYLD (apitegromab) for spinal muscular atrophy. On the call, management said "in error" that the FDA required no post-marketing safety commitments on bone or reproductive safety. The filing says the FDA did require a pregnancy safety study, and asked the company to report all fracture cases within 15 days as "alert reports".

## Key numbers and dates (quoted)
- Call date: "On September 14, 2026"
- Approval scope: "the U.S. Food and Drug Administration ("FDA") has approved ISEMBYLD™ (apitegromab-mstn) for the treatment of spinal muscular atrophy in adults and children 2 years of age and older who are currently receiving a survival motor neuron 2 (SMN2)-targeted treatment"
- Correction: "the Company's management stated in error that the FDA did not require any post-marketing safety commitments regarding bone or reproductive safety of ISEMBYLD"
- Requirement: "a post-marketing requirement that the Company establish a pregnancy safety study to collect and analyze information on pregnancy complications and birth outcomes for exposed pregnancies"
- Fracture reports: "submit all serious and non-serious domestic and/or foreign cases of fractures as 15-day "alert reports.""
