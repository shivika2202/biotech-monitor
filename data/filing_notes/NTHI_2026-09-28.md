## What it says
NeOnc says it is scheduled to meet the FDA in person on November 17, 2026, for an End-of-Phase 1 meeting about NEO212. At the meeting it will ask for feedback on the Phase 2 design, and on whether a trial could support accelerated approval. The release repeats the Phase 1 dose findings and describes two individual patients. It states that scheduling the meeting "does not establish FDA agreement".

## Key numbers and dates (quoted)
- Meeting: "November 17, 2026, for an in-person End-of-Phase 1 Type B meeting concerning NEO212", at "FDA's White Oak campus in Silver Spring, Maryland"
- Prior FDA step: "In September 2025, FDA authorized the Company to proceed with the Phase 2a and Phase 2b portions of NEO212-01"
- Dosing: "dose escalation was discontinued following a dose-limiting toxicity at 810 mg, administered once daily on Days 1 through 5 of a 28-day cycle, and that 610 mg was selected as the recommended Phase 2 dose"
- Patient 1 (recurrent IDH1 wild-type, MGMT-methylated glioblastoma): "a partial response, including an approximately 60% reduction in tumor size, followed by prolonged disease control lasting 21 treatment months"
- Patient 2 (lung cancer metastatic to the brain): "stable disease lasting approximately 16 months"
- Product: "NEO212 is an investigational oral conjugate of temozolomide and NEO100"

## Source type
Company-reported (press release furnished as an 8-K exhibit). The FDA meeting date has not been confirmed by the FDA.

## Promotional language and missing numbers
- **Promotional:** "encouraging clinical activity"; "This duration of disease control is notable".
- **Two-patient stories:** the efficacy information is two single-patient cases. The release itself says they "do not establish efficacy and require confirmation in larger studies".
- **Missing:** how many patients were treated in Phase 1; the response rate across all patients; adverse event counts; and what the dose-limiting toxicity was.
- **Balanced wording also present:** "its scheduling does not establish FDA agreement on a trial design, an accelerated approval pathway or eventual approval"; "We may update our plans after evaluating the agency's feedback".

## Unverified / unclear
- **unverified:** the November 17, 2026 FDA meeting date. This is a catalyst date reported only by the company, and the release says "the meeting may be rescheduled or its format changed".
- **unverified:** the September 2025 FDA authorization of the Phase 2a/2b portions (company statement only).
- **unverified:** both individual patient outcomes. No publication or trial registry results are cited.
