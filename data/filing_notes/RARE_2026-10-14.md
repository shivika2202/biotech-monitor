# RARE 8-K filed 2026-10-14

- **Company:** Ultragenyx Pharmaceutical Inc. (CIK 1515673)
- **Filing date:** 2026-10-14 (period of report 2026-10-08)
- **Items:** 1.01 (entry into a material definitive agreement)
- **Source:** 8-K https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1515673/000119312526421079/rare-20261008.htm
- **Main exhibit:** none attached. The 8-K says the agreement "will be filed with the Company's Annual Report on Form 10-K for the year ended December 31, 2026".
- **Notes written:** 2026-10-15

## What it says
Ultragenyx agreed to sell a second Rare Pediatric Disease Priority Review Voucher for $203 million in cash, paid at closing. It received this voucher when the FDA approved FAYUVI (UX111) for MPS IIIA (Sanfilippo syndrome Type A). The sale still has to clear an antitrust waiting period.

## Key numbers and dates (quoted)
- Agreement date: "On October 8, 2026"
- Voucher origin: "awarded the PRV by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration ("FDA") on September 17, 2026 upon approval of FAYUVI TM (rebisufligene etisparvovec-hopf) also known as UX111, for the treatment of pediatric patients with mucopolysaccharidosis type IIIA (MPS IIIA, Sanfilippo syndrome Type A)"
- Price: "the buyer agreed to pay the Company $203 million, payable in cash, upon the closing of the transaction"
- Condition: "the expiration or termination of the applicable waiting period under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976"
