## What it says
Dyne released one-year data from the dose-escalation part of its Phase 1/2 ACHIEVE trial of z-basivarsen (DYNE-101) in myotonic dystrophy type 1 (DM1). The data pool 25 to 26 patients across three dose groups. Patients improved from their own starting point on hand myotonia, function, strength and a patient questionnaire, and some measures were also compared with a matched natural history group. The company says topline data from the registrational cohort are expected in Q1 2027, and a possible BLA for accelerated approval in Q3 2027. The data are in Item 8.01, which is "filed", as well as in the furnished press release.

## Key numbers and dates (quoted)
- Pooled group: "N=25-26" from the "3.4 mg/kg every four weeks, 5.4 mg/kg every 8 weeks and 6.8 mg/kg every 8 weeks cohorts"
- Limitation: "only 8 of 26 participants in the pooled dose group received the registrational dose for the full 12 months analyzed, and most did not receive the registrational dose during this timeframe"
- Comparison group: "(N=41-46) from the ongoing END-DM1 natural history study who were propensity-matched"
- vHOT: "mean baseline vHOT of 8.2 seconds, which decreased by 3.2 seconds at 6 months (standard deviation of change from baseline: 3.5 seconds). This is compared to an increase of 0.4 seconds in the placebo group from the ACHIEVE MAD at 6 months (N=14), representing a 3.6 second relative improvement"
- Function: "participants improved on 5xSTS by 1.2 seconds and on 10MWR by 0.3 seconds relative to baseline"; "5xSTS was not included in the END-DM1 natural history study, so no comparisons could be made"
- Strength: "improved on both QMT total and hand grip by 4.8% predicted relative to baseline"
- Questionnaire: "improved on the MDHI total score by 25.2% relative to baseline, showing a divergence from an unmatched END-DM1 natural history cohort (N=410)"
- Safety: "no related serious treatment emergent adverse events were identified"; data "as of April 20, 2026"
- Registrational cohort: "The mean baseline value for vHOT of the 71 participants enrolled in the ACHIEVE REC is 8.3 seconds (standard deviation at baseline: 6.0 seconds)"
- Timeline: "topline data are expected in the first quarter of 2027 to support a potential Biologics License Application ("BLA") submission for U.S. Accelerated Approval in the third quarter of 2027"
- Phase 3: HARMONIA "currently recruiting participants at over 35 active global sites"
- Trial ID: "NCT05481879"

## Source type
Company-reported. Item 8.01 is "filed"; the press release is furnished. The data were also presented at the World Muscle Society congress and the AANEM meeting. Those presentations are company-prepared and not peer reviewed.

## Promotional language and missing numbers
- **Promotional:** "favorable safety profile"; "further support our confidence"; "We attribute these results to the differentiated properties of our FORCE platform".
- **Missing:** p-values and confidence intervals for every measure; the size of the "divergence" from the natural history group on 10MWR, QMT and MDHI (described, not quantified); adverse event counts; and the number of patients in each dose group.
- **Uncontrolled comparisons:** most results are compared with the patients' own starting values, not with placebo. The questionnaire comparison uses an "unmatched" natural history group.

## Unverified / unclear
- **unverified:** the "Q1 2027" topline data and "Q3 2027" BLA timing. These are company plans and could change.
- **unverified:** the FDA Breakthrough Therapy, Orphan Drug and Fast Track designations (company statement only).
- **unclear:** the vHOT comparison mixes groups. The 6-month comparison uses the pooled dose group (N=25-26) against the placebo group (N=14), and the release does not say how many pooled patients had 6-month vHOT data.
