# SRPT 8-K filed 2026-09-23

- **Company:** Sarepta Therapeutics, Inc. (CIK 873303)
- **Filing date:** 2026-09-23 (period of report 2026-09-23)
- **Items:** 1.01 (entry into a material definitive agreement)
- **Source:** 8-K https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/873303/000119312526399416/srpt-20260923.htm
- **Main exhibit:** none attached. The 8-K says Sarepta "intends to file" the agreement "as an exhibit to its Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q for the quarterly period ending September 30, 2026".
- **Notes written:** 2026-10-15

## What it says
Sarepta settled patent lawsuits with REGENXBIO and the University of Pennsylvania over gene therapy patents, including the claims involving ELEVIDYS. Catalent was also a party to the settlement. Sarepta will pay REGENXBIO $39.0 million. In return, REGENXBIO and Penn release past infringement claims and agree not to sue over Sarepta products that use the AAVrh74 capsid, the virus shell used to deliver the gene. The cases will be dismissed with prejudice, and no party admits liability.

## Key numbers and dates (quoted)
- Effective date: "On September 23, 2026"
- Payment: "Sarepta will pay REGENXBIO a lump sum of $39.0 million within ten days of the Effective Date"
- Prior accrual: "in the three and six months ended June 30, 2026, the Company recorded a litigation contingency charge of $39.0 million"
- Patents: "U.S. Patent No. 11,680,274", "U.S. Patent No. 10,526,617" and "U.S. Patent No. 9,198,984 and certain related patents"
- Scope: "any Sarepta gene therapy product that uses the AAVrh74-based capsid, including ELEVIDYS"
- Outcome: "dismissal, withdrawal or other disposal of the Litigations with prejudice"; "does not include any admission of liability or wrongdoing by any party"
