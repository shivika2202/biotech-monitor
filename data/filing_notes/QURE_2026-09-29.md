## What it says
uniQure released 36-month and 48-month data from its Phase I/II studies of AMT-130 (ifezuntirgene inilparvovec), a one-time gene therapy for Huntington's disease. At 48 months, the pre-specified primary measure (cUHDRS) showed 44% slowing compared with an external control group, but this "did not reach statistical significance (p=0.144)". The company argues the 48-month effect is understated because data in the comparison group were incomplete. It says the BLA (its application for FDA approval) was already submitted using earlier 36-month data, and that these new results "were not part of the submission".

## Key numbers and dates (quoted)
- Data cutoff: "a data cutoff of June 30, 2026"
- Patients: "Twenty-nine patients have been treated ... (n=17 high dose; n=12 low dose)"; "The 48-month analysis includes 12 patients at each dose"
- 48-month primary endpoint: "cUHDRS showed a 44% slowing of disease progression compared to the updated external control and did not reach statistical significance (p=0.144)"
- 48-month TFC: "61% slowing of disease progression compared to the updated external control (nominal p=0.008)"
- 36-month (n=15): "cUHDRS showed 80% slowing of disease progression compared to the updated external control (nominal p=0.005)"; "TFC showed 67% slowing ... (nominal p=0.011)"
- Footnote: "cUHDRS is the pre-specified primary endpoint for the 48 months analysis; all other p-values are nominal"; "Percentage slowing was sensitive to the magnitude of decline in the external control"
- Control: "propensity score-matched external controls (n=1,337 for high dose) from an updated ENROLL-HD natural history dataset"; missing data "reaching 53% in the updated ENROLL-HD matched controls at 48 months"
- Post hoc: "53.5% slowing based on cUHDRS (nominal p=0.041) and 68.3% based on TFC (nominal p=0.001)" (the headline rounds these to 54% and 68%)
- FDA: "At the June 2026 Type B meeting, the FDA communicated that the 36-month data from 12 high-dose patients would be acceptable as the primary basis for the BLA submission under the accelerated approval pathway, and the submission was made accordingly"
- Safety: "five high dose participants (17%) experienced a treatment-related serious adverse event (SAE) related to central nervous system inflammation, all of which fully resolved"; "one suicide in a low-dose patient occurred, approximately five years after receiving treatment. This was assessed as unrelated to treatment by the study investigator"
- Trial IDs: "NCT05243017, NCT04120493"

## Source type
Company-reported (press release and slides, furnished). There is one quote from an outside physician, Victor Sung, M.D. (UAB), but it appears inside the company's release, so it is not an independent source. The data are not yet peer reviewed; the company says it will present them "at a future scientific meeting".

## Promotional language and missing numbers
- **Promotional:** the headline "Showing Continued Slowing of Disease Progression"; "These positive data are meaningful"; "a leading gene therapy company"; "delivering on the promise of gene therapy".
- **How the results are framed:** the headline leads with the slowing percentages. The fact that the 48-month primary endpoint was not statistically significant appears only in parentheses ("non-significant p=0.144").
- **No independent check of the bias argument:** the claim that the control group "likely understated" decline is the company's own analysis ("The Company believes"). No independent review is cited.
- **Missing:** the date the BLA was submitted; whether the FDA has accepted the BLA for review; any FDA decision date; and confidence intervals.

## Unverified / unclear
- **unverified:** the BLA submission and any FDA review timeline. The filing gives no submission date and no FDA action date, so no catalyst date can be stated from this filing.
- **unverified:** the FDA's June 2026 Type B meeting feedback (company account only).
- **unclear:** the 48-month high-dose numbers differ within the same release. One section gives the cUHDRS change as "-0.90" and the TFC change as "-0.37". The dose comparison section gives "–0.91" and "−0.30". The release does not explain the difference.
- **unclear:** the press release gives the external control as "n=1,337 for high dose". The slide text shows "Enroll-HD B (N=634)", "(N=633)" and "(N=631)" for the cUHDRS component tests. The filing does not explain the difference.
- **unclear:** a slide reports "Slowed HD progression by 113% based on SWRT". The filing does not explain what slowing above 100% means.
